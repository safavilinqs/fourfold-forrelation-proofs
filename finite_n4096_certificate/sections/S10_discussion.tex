\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Interpretation}

Theorem~\ref{thm:main} gives a strict finite-budget separation under one physical interaction meter. A coherent active circuit succeeds with six charged sample traversals, while every classically adaptive number-sector-incoherent fresh-batch passive strategy of charge six fails, despite arbitrary idlers, within-sector quantum states, collective measurements, outcome width, classical computation, and branch-dependent dose allocation.

The mechanism is temporal. The active photon carries phase information from one unknown sign bank through a public transformation and into a second unknown bank before measurement. A passive tree may accumulate arbitrary classical information from previous batches, while its later quantum amplitudes are prepared fresh. The direct-sum adaptive proof shows that outcome selection changes the correlated law on complete temporal histories without increasing the normalized feature mass or the physical occurrence coefficients.

The finite proof complements the provisional asymptotic $\Omega(N^{1/8})$ passive floor for the broader full fresh-probe model~\cite{SafaviNaeini2026}. The asymptotic exponent identifies an unbounded separation, while the $q=64$ certificate determines that the separation is already strict at $N=4096$ for the number-sector-incoherent subclass after all physical masks, conditioning costs, coefficient constants, and adaptive quantifiers are retained.

\subsection{Claim boundary}

The passive theorem covers classical memory between fresh quantum batches that are block diagonal in total signal photon number. It excludes both coherent quantum memory or coherent control passed between batches and coherence between distinct signal-number sectors within a fresh batch. The latter exclusion is essential to the current certificate: 272 unbalanced high-sector profile-split/state incidences, forming 136 different-number occupation edges, were found during this audit, and their physical coefficients remain unproved. Intermediate models with number-sector coherence, bounded coherent memory, staggered charged interactions inside a nominal round, or restricted inter-batch quantum channels require separate definitions and proofs. Query-adaptivity and quantum-memory separations in other models provide conceptual comparisons~\cite{GirishEtAl2024,AharonovCotlerQi2022,ChenEtAl2022}; their inequalities are not used in the present certificate.

The theorem does not determine the optimal active dose, the optimal passive dose, or the smallest dimension at which a finite separation holds. It does not establish the same passive lower bound when fresh probes have coherence between total signal-number sectors. Active dose five remains open. The existing $N=1024$ ledger is diagnostic and does not establish a theorem. The signed-permutation hard pair is a proof witness and does not prescribe an experimental input distribution.

\subsection{Experimental boundary}

The active resource row is exact at the ideal-circuit level, including the four sign banks, logical mode dimension, path dimension, three photons, two charged traversals per photon, receiver, majority error, and absence of postselection. The scalar robustness condition gives a necessary implementation target within the stated symmetric model. A device-specific error theorem requires measured component data and a noise model that treats loss, leakage, phase, sign errors, dark counts, multiphoton events, and correlations between the three flags.

The current evidence assessment supports a theory-paper statement with explicit experimental requirements. A smaller $N=1024$ theorem would reduce each sign bank to 1024 coordinates and the total sign count to 4096, but it would still require a dependency-exact outward one-batch certificate below $1/3-10^{-3}$ and a device satisfying the same active contrast inequality. That project is distinct from the completed $N=4096$ proof.

\subsection{Reproducibility}

The complete source history is included because the finite certificate depends on a large but finite collection of structural coefficient theorems, independent audits, exact rational bounds, and directed numerical assembly. The manuscript identifies the accepted dependency chain, while the source snapshot preserves rejected routes and counterexamples that prevent superseded inequalities from re-entering the proof through cumulative bookkeeping. The focused and full commands in Sec.~\ref{sec:verification} provide the operational record for the theorem statement printed here.
