\section{The adaptive lift}
\label{sec:adaptive}

\subsection{Strategy factorization}

Fix terminal weights in $[-1,1]$ for the signed transcript functional of a finite passive tree. A complete temporal ket history is denoted by $I$, a complete temporal bra history by $J$, and the signed strategy coefficient after removing the oracle characters by $D(I,J)$.

Outcome-dependent fresh probes can correlate complete histories because a later preparation may depend on every earlier measurement result. By convexity, a state satisfying Eq.~\eqref{eq:model-number-sector} may first be refined into a classical mixture of pure probes, each supported in one fixed total signal-number sector; revealing that mixture label to the controller can only enlarge the passive class. The following factorization retains the resulting correlations while showing that classical outcome selection cannot create additional normalized interference mass: every mixture label and child is placed in an orthogonal direct-sum sector, and normalization and POVM completeness bound the total squared feature mass by one.

\begin{lemma}[Normalized strategy factorization]
\label{lem:adaptive-factorization}
There are Hilbert-space features $u_I,v_J$ such that
\begin{align}
D(I,J)&=\langle v_J,u_I\rangle,
\\
\sum_I\|u_I\|^2&\le1,
&
\sum_J\|v_J\|^2&\le1.
\label{eq:adaptive-invariant}
\end{align}
The statement holds for arbitrary outcome width, depth, rare outcomes, outcome-selected number-sector-incoherent child probes, and branch-dependent dose partitions.
\end{lemma}

Conditional on its refined classical mixture label, write the first fresh probe as

\begin{equation}
\ket{\psi}=\sum_i\ket{i}\ket{\alpha_i},
\qquad
\sum_i\|\alpha_i\|^2=1,
\end{equation}

where every occupied basis label $i$ in this sum has the same total signal photon number. Taking one additional direct sum over the classical mixture labels preserves the normalization below.

and let $\{E_y\}_y$ be the root POVM. Define

\begin{equation}
a_{y,i}=E_y^{1/2}(\ket{i}\ket{\alpha_i}).
\end{equation}

POVM completeness gives $\sum_{y,i}\|a_{y,i}\|^2=1$. If the child selected by outcome $y$ has features $u_{y,I},v_{y,J}$, define

\begin{equation}
U_{i,I}=\bigoplus_y a_{y,i}\otimes u_{y,I},
\qquad
V_{j,J}=\bigoplus_y a_{y,j}\otimes v_{y,J}.
\label{eq:adaptive-direct-sum}
\end{equation}

The direct sum reproduces the complete signed coefficient and satisfies

\begin{equation}
\sum_{i,I}\|U_{i,I}\|^2
\le\sum_{y,i}\|a_{y,i}\|^2=1,
\end{equation}

with the same bound for the column features. Rare outcomes introduce no inverse probability because the proof does not normalize posterior laws.

\subsection{Temporal occupation pullback}

For a temporal history $I=(i_1,\ldots,i_d)$, define its aggregate physical Fock occupation

\begin{equation}
f(I)=i_1+\cdots+i_d.
\end{equation}

Diagonal sign characters multiply according to

\begin{equation}
\chi_{i_1}(x)\cdots\chi_{i_d}(x)=\chi_{f(I)}(x).
\label{eq:adaptive-character}
\end{equation}

Repeated physical modes remain in the integer occupation and reduce through parity in the character. Orthogonality of the refined number-sector labels makes the strategy cross-Gram symbol zero unless ket and bra histories have equal total signal photon number in every batch, and hence $|f(I)|=|f(J)|$ in every contributing term. The branchwise hard-dose condition gives $|f(I)|\le6$. Every adaptive hard-instance moment is therefore a pullback of the same balanced degree-four through degree-twelve physical kernels certified in Sec.~\ref{sec:one-batch}.

Duplicating a physical row or column through several temporal decompositions does not increase a Hilbert factorization norm. Setting $p_I=\|U_I\|^2$ and $w_J=\|V_J\|^2$ produces two generally correlated laws with total mass at most one, which are within the arbitrary-law scope of Eq.~\eqref{eq:onebatch-coefficient}.

\subsection{The Perron interface}

Push $p$ and $w$ forward to the four-block occupations of $f(I)$ and $f(J)$. For the same nonnegative symmetric 210-state matrix $B$, the complete occurrence sum has the form $x^TBy$, where $\|x\|_2,\|y\|_2\le1$. Hence

\begin{equation}
x^TBy\le\|B\|_{2\to2}=\rho(B).
\end{equation}

The outward Collatz--Wielandt certificate therefore accepts the two independent laws generated by the strategy features within each total-number block. The adaptive occurrence multiplier is exactly one.

Promise conditioning is applied once to the completed unconditioned transcript comparison, giving the same total as Eq.~\eqref{eq:statement-total}. Lemma~\ref{lem:adaptive-factorization} covers classical feed-forward between number-sector-incoherent fresh batches. It does not extend to coherent quantum systems retained between batches or to fresh probes with coherence between different total signal-number sectors.
