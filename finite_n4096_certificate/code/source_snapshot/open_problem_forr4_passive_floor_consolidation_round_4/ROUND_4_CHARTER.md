## Operating principles

- A number below $1/3$ is a theorem only when every sector, physical
  coefficient, conditioning cost, and adaptive quantifier is controlled.
- A one-batch lower bound is an intermediate gate, not the adaptive theorem.
- Negative results should trigger a documented pivot, not repeated local
  polishing.
- A physical coefficient vector that kills the independent scalar ledger is
  a proof-route obstruction, not a passive protocol and not a disproof of the
  hard instance.
- The earlier 712- and 808-entry routing narratives are conditional historical calculations, not accepted ledger states.
- The coefficient-one quarantine, the later 80-entry residual inventory, and the separate dual-endpoint caveat are closed.
- Preserve exact branchwise hard-dose accounting.
- Do not optimize active dose five or the asymptotic exponent unless doing so
  directly improves the paper's finite-size separation.
- Do not call a regime experimentally feasible without listing its actual
  mode count and operations.
