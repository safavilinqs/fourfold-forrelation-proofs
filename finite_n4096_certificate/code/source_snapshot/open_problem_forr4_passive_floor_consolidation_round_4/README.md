# Passive four-forrelation consolidation: round 4

Date initialized: 2026-07-15

## Purpose

Round 4 is the finite-size theorem and paper-closeout phase:

> Resolve the distinctness-masked q64 contraction, rebuild the finite-size theorem ledger from valid coefficients, and then close one rigorous active--passive separation or pivot the witness.

The initial global decision retained $N=4096$ as the candidate size. A later audit found that the universal coefficient-one lemma omits physical cross-cut distinctness masks. Independent replacement theorems prove all 354 affected entries, the final residual contraction proves the later 80 entries, and an independent audit certifies the final 12 dual-endpoint entries. The machine-readable registry supports all 888 balanced entries. A dependency-exact outward certificate gives total $0.268858135059926<1/3-10^{-3}$ at fixed rational attenuation $\beta=19/25$. The adaptive tree-frontier theorem proves a globally normalized direct-sum factorization with multiplier one for unrestricted classical feed-forward between fresh quantum batches. Thus passive hard dose six is excluded at $N=4096$ in the declared model. The concise paper theorem package is complete. The dated hardware screen finds the ideal active implementation not yet experimentally credible because the required $4096$-dimensional coherent transform has not been demonstrated at the $0.9042949$ contrast threshold.

The inherited asymptotic theorem is

$$
D_{\mathsf P}^{\rm hard}=\Omega(N^{1/12}),
\qquad
D_{\mathsf A}^{\rm hard}\le6.
$$

The asymptotic theorem is background for the paper, not the lead Round 4 optimization target.

The historical exact compatible-law baseline is documented in
`notes/JOINT_PHYSICAL_LAW_DIAGNOSTIC.md`. It activates all 16 leading
families and measures nontrivial shared-law cancellation at orders four and
32. It did not decide the q32 route and is retained as research history. The
masked-universal audit now defines the accepted checkpoint.

## Why a new round

Round 3 made two decisive advances:

- it improved the passive floor from $N^{1/24}$ to $N^{1/12}$; and
- it proved that the immediate next grouped graph-norm route is
  exponent-sharp.

It did not turn the separation into a constant-carrying finite-size
statement suitable for the paper.  That is the live Round 4 question.
Active dose five and the optimal passive exponent are deferred unless they
directly affect the paper claim.
