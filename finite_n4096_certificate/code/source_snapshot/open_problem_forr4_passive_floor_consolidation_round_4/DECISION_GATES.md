# Round 4 decision gates

## Gate 1 — theorem labels

Every quantitative statement must be labeled:

- **proved:** complete analytic argument with scoped regressions;
- **certified numeric:** rigorous rational or interval certificate;
- **diagnostic:** useful computation without theorem-level coverage;
- **protocol:** physically realizable strategy with dose and error;
- **relaxation:** upper or lower optimization surrogate; or
- **conjecture:** unsupported target.

Diagnostics and relaxations must never be added to proved transcript budgets.

## Gate 4 — experimental credibility

A mathematically certified size enters the paper headline only after
EXPERIMENTAL_SCORECARD.md states:

- $N_*$, $M=4N_*$, and all ancillary modes;
- state preparation, transformations, six charged traversals, and receiver;
- conditioning or promise-preparation cost;
- loss, phase, detector, and repetition assumptions; and
- which assumptions are theorem-level and which are implementation
  idealizations.

Decision: the resource statement and primary-evidence screen are complete, but present-day credibility fails at $N=4096$. With a nominal 98% detector the remaining non-detector geometric-mean loss budget is only about 0.35 dB, and no reviewed platform demonstration supplies the required $4096$-dimensional coherent Sylvester transform at the total contrast threshold. This blocks an experimental headline but does not invalidate the certified finite-size theory result.

## Gate 5 — hard-instance pivot

separation in the declared finite-size window.  Pivot when:

- a certified physical coefficient consumes the available margin;
- the adaptive lift loses the one-batch margin;

- a replacement witness wins the same finite-size scorecard.

A replacement must preserve the exact task, access class, branchwise hard
dose, constant promise separation, and the active six-dose protocol.

Current decision: retain the signed-permutation witness for the theory paper. If experimental credibility is mandatory, open a separate bounded $N=1024$ theorem-and-platform upgrade rather than reopening closed $q=64$ coefficient families.

## Gate 7 — deferred long-horizon work

Do not reopen terminal-shape enumeration or arbitrary grouped graph norms.

asymptotic exponent unless the work directly rescues the finite-size paper
claim.  Any asymptotic project must identify which assumption of the Round 3

- arbitrary grouped unit vectors;

Without such an escape mechanism, the project is stopped.

## Gate 8 — protocol claims used for falsification

A passive or active protocol claim must include:

- full state preparation;
- all oracle/sample traversals;
- receiver or POVM;
- classical and quantum memory assumptions;
- branchwise worst-case dose;
- exact or certified error on both promise sides; and
- a reproducible implementation or symbolic certificate.

A tester, PPT, separable, or symmetry relaxation is not a protocol unless a
rounding theorem constructs one.
