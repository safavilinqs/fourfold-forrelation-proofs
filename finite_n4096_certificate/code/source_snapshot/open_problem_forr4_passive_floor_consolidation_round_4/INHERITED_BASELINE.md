# Inherited baseline

Date frozen: 2026-07-15

## Proved facts

For the exact constant-margin task and unrestricted classically adaptive
passive model:

$$
D_{\mathsf P}^{\rm hard}=\Omega(N^{1/12}),
\qquad
D_{\mathsf A}^{\rm hard}\le6.
\tag{0.1}
$$

The passive theorem follows from the complete four-initial-state terminal
image and exact centered repairs.  The image has 236 reachable states, 39
terminal types, and 22 sensitive types.  Every sensitive level from five
through twelve has \(N^{-1}\) decay after repair; level four retains
\(N^{-1/2}\).

The complete inherited-plus-Round-3 regression suite passes.

## Proved framework limitation

The unique sensitive level-twelve type is a three-path forest.  For a legal
paired-path physical placement, the accepted grouped projective upper bound
is \(O(N^{-1})\), while explicit grouped unit vectors give

$$
N^{-1}(1-N^{-1})^2(1-2N^{-1})^3.
\tag{0.2}
$$

At \(N=1024\), this equals \(0.000968956354\), or
\(0.9922113065N^{-1}\).  The canonical coefficient family has 12 initial
triples and 1,080 positive all-fresh histories.

Therefore arbitrary grouped graph norms cannot improve the \(1/12\)
exponent.  This does not exclude a gain from physically realizable posterior
frames, pre-norm cancellation, or another hard instance.

## Finite-size evidence, not a theorem

For the attenuated signed-permutation plant at \(N=1024\):

- conditioned degree-eight total: \(0.2482247496\);
- accepted partial total through proved high sectors: \(0.2797585469\);
- coarse completion diagnostic: \(0.3331326055<1/3\);
- diagnostic margin: \(0.0002007278\); and
- unresolved balanced entries: 848.

The completion diagnostic uses provisional charges.  It is not a one-batch
certificate and cannot be lifted to adaptivity.

## Active boundary evidence

The known active protocol uses hard dose six.  Two complete folded-chain
flags do not reach error \(1/3\) at \(N=1024\), even with an arbitrary
collective measurement:

$$
\text{optimal error}=0.3611610554.
\tag{0.3}
$$

A genuinely interleaved five-traversal circuit and two flags plus one
coherent extra query remain open.

## Missing evidence

Round 4 inherits no proof of:

- adaptive stability of the finite-size signed-permutation ledger;
- optimality of active dose six;
- optimality of the current hard instance; or
- asymptotic optimality of the \(N^{1/12}\) passive floor.
