## Frozen task

Round 4 uses the same task, access class, and meter as the original program:

- $N=2^d$, with $N=4096$ the live theorem target and $N=1024$ the preferred
  smaller-size upgrade target;
- distinguish $F_{4,H}\ge1/4$ from $F_{4,H}\le-1/4$;
- unrestricted classically adaptive passive trees with arbitrary fresh
  quantum batches, idlers, repeated modes, entanglement, vacuum coherence,
  collective POVMs, and classical feed-forward; and
- branchwise hard photon-pass dose, with every traversal charged.

The initial feasibility window is

$$
N\in\{256,512,1024,2048,4096\},
\qquad M=4N.
$$

These are targets to evaluate, not an unsupported claim of hardware
feasibility.  The final paper must state the required mode count, active and
passive hard dose, error margin, conditioning loss, and experimental
operations explicitly.

The current signed-permutation witness uses $N=q^2$ with power-of-two
$q$, so within this generic task window its implemented sizes are
$N=256,1024,4096$. The other sizes require a rectangular or replacement
witness; this is an implementation restriction, not a restriction on the
task.

Changing the promise, weakening the passive model, using expected dose, or
proving only a one-batch surrogate does not establish the headline
unrestricted separation.  A restricted experimental corollary may be useful
only if it is labeled separately and physically justified.

## Primary completion criterion

Produce a theorem/corollary package containing:

1. one explicit $N$ in the feasibility window, currently targeting $4096$;
2. a rigorous lower bound excluding every unrestricted adaptive passive
   protocol of hard dose six;
3. the active six-dose protocol in the identical model and meter;
4. a certified error or transcript-distance margin, not just strict
   inequality at numerical precision;
5. all finite-size constants and promise-conditioning costs; and
6. a short experimental resource table.

If no $N$ in the initial window passes, determine the smallest certified
$N$, explain whether it is experimentally credible, and pivot the hard
instance rather than polishing an unusable asymptotic theorem.

## Deprioritized questions

For this paper, Round 4 does not need:

- optimal active dose or an active dose-five result;
- the optimal passive asymptotic exponent;
- a complete passive protocol frontier; or
- a general classification of alternative hard instances.

These remain falsification and pivot tools.  The inherited
$\Omega(N^{1/12})$ theorem is sufficient asymptotic context unless a new
mechanism directly improves the finite-size paper result.

## Honest strong outcomes short of completion

The full goal remains active until a paper-ready finite-size separation is
certified.
Meaningful intermediate outcomes include:

- a certified one-batch dose-six pass or fail;
- a rigorous obstruction forcing abandonment of the current hard instance;
- a competitive passive protocol above or below six;
- a replacement hard instance that wins a common quantitative scorecard; or
- an experimentally justified restricted corollary, clearly separated from
  the unrestricted theorem.
