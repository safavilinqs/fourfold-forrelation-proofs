# Open problems and theorem targets

Date: 2026-07-15

The open problems are grouped by contribution to the broader mission.  The
first five retain their original numbers so existing notes remain valid.

## Umbrella questions

The numbered problems below serve five program questions:

- **Q1 — Scale:** what is the strongest correct asymptotic passive floor?
- **Q2 — Finite size:** can passive dose six be excluded near \(N=1024\)?
- **Q3 — Algorithmic boundary:** how good can valid passive protocols be, and
  is active dose six optimal or merely the first construction?
- **Q4 — Mechanism:** which resource creates the active advantage and which
  posterior-stable inequality blocks passive emulation?
- **Q5 — Robustness:** does the separation persist across hard instances, or
  is the current witness unusually favorable or unfavorable?

OP3-1 through OP3-5 primarily address Q2; OP3-6 and OP3-7 address Q1 and Q4;
OP3-8 through OP3-10 address Q3 and Q5.  Each resolved problem should update
the corresponding row of `MISSION_LEDGER.md`.

### OP3-7 — Posterior-stable passive contraction

Prove or sharply falsify the level-sensitive posterior contraction

$$
C^v(1+D)^vN^{-\max\{1/2,v/16\}}
$$

after signed terminal terms at level \(v\) are grouped, uniformly under
adaptive outcome selection.  The first bounded fork asked whether the
checked \(\sigma=1\), \(v\ge9\) interface family occurs in the true
interpolation image; the decision below replaces that broad question with
an exact terminal test case.

First bounded decision: structural exclusion fails.  The old relaxed star
violates the true boundary-degree cap, but six all-new legal transfers
produce a sensitive level-twelve forest of three four-layer paths.  Its
local branching weight is positive.  A legal placement with one paired path
and two singleton paths has \(\sigma_{\rm ass}=1\).  Thus neither absence
from the terminal image nor scalar branching signs provide the missing
factor.

Second bounded decision: the exact three-path gate passes.  Keeping the
entire frame skeleton in the grouped-entry projective norm gives
\(N^{-1}\): the paired path costs at most one and the two singleton paths
each give \(N^{-1/2}\).  This beats the required \(N^{-3/4}\) and avoids the
invalid RT-003 conversion because no Hilbert auxiliary is later treated
projectively.

Third bounded decision: exhaustive enumeration finds 222 reachable states,
34 terminal types, and eight sensitive high-level types.  Five pass the
\(N^{1/16}\) target.  Every level-eleven/twelve type gains \(N^{-1}\), which
improves the general passive floor to \(\Omega(N^{1/20})\).  Three joint
saturators retain exponent \(1/2\) under both safe routes: two reflected
connected level-nine trees and one level-ten \(6+4\) forest.

Fourth bounded decision: the level-ten forest is removed.  All 200 legal
histories force one odd centered derivative at the same middle vertex.  Its
exact Stein re-expansion has best safe decay one on all 282 dangerous
original partitions and 5,295 retained extensions.  The general passive
floor first improves to \(\Omega(N^{1/18})\).

Fifth bounded decision: both reflected level-nine trees are removed.  All
200 histories for the upper-branching representative force an odd centered
outer derivative.  Exact scoring covers seven dangerous partitions, 2,906
Type-A branch cases, every reflected retained Type-B case, and the 1,905
all-fresh Type-B placements that cancel.  The current passive floor is
therefore first \(\Omega(N^{1/16})\).

Sixth bounded decision: the complete low-level image is resolved.  Adding
both potential-eight initial collisions and the potential-four path gives

types have decay \(N^{-1}\).  The unique level-seven/six saturators have a
forced odd centered factor in all 36/four histories, and every retained

Seventh bounded decision: the limiting grouped graph norm is exponent-sharp.
For a legal paired-path placement, explicit grouped unit vectors retain

$$

$$

after every same-layer distinctness mask.  This matches the accepted

retains more than 99.2 percent of the bare \(N^{-1}\) value.

Current bounded target: the complete terminal list and the arbitrary-grouped
