# Transposed dominant-class witness and hybrid promise repair

Date: 2026-07-15

Status: certified numerical physical witness, proved hybrid concentration
theorem, and repaired route decision.  The result decides the leading scalar
gate but does not complete the one-batch ledger or adaptive lower bound.

## 1. Decision result

After the first mixed-orbit witness and finite-tilt promise repair, the
leading unforced split orbit was generated by

$$
(3,1,1,5):(1,0,1,3)
$$

and its complement and reversals.  Reoptimizing the independent scalar
ledger allowed coefficient

$$
c_* = 0.0139022838042.
\tag{1.1}
$$

One valid physical law has a certified coefficient lower

$$
\boxed{c_{\rm phys}\ge 0.0142810242047>c_*.}
\tag{1.2}
$$

With the endpoint finite-tilt promise bound, inserting only this certified

$$
\beta=0.779473797581,
\qquad
\text{total}=0.333360945553,
\tag{1.3}
$$

above \(1/3\) by \(2.76122\times10^{-5}\).  Thus the endpoint-tilt scalar
completion is physically obstructed.

The obstruction is repaired analytically by continuing the Chernoff tilt
and switching only the first martingale block to its global Kearns--Saul

$$
\boxed{

\qquad

\qquad
\text{slack}=0.000494024345.}
\tag{1.4}
$$

Decision: retain the signed-permutation scalar route, but advance to the
newly ranked profile family.  Neither physical witness may be removed from
future baselines.

## 2. The physical law and symmetry reduction

Put both endpoint pairs on all nonzero vertical differences and put the
quintic triple in one hidden column.  For the transposed endpoint split, the
row Gram decomposes by:

1. endpoint xor \(h\);
2. zero/nonzero hidden-column Fourier frequencies for the cubic and quintic
   factors; and
3. zero/nonzero twisted common-row translation frequency.

A final block is indexed by

$$
(x,\delta,r)\in
(\mathbb F_2^5\setminus\{0\})^2\times\mathbb F_2^5
$$

and has ambient size

$$
q(q-1)^2=30{,}752.
$$

Direct dense calculations give coefficients \(0.0554262493\),
\(0.0825349367\), and \(0.0401545711\) at \(q=4,8,16\).  These values are
nonmonotone and were not extrapolated.

## 3. PSD lower compression

Let \(M\succeq0\) be one final Gram block and let \(J\) be any selected
column set.  The generalized Schur complement gives

$$
M_{:,J}M_{J,J}^{+}M_{J,:}\preceq M.
\tag{3.1}
$$

Therefore the trace square root of any factor below the left-hand side is a
valid lower witness; exact full-rank reconstruction is unnecessary.

The \(r=0\) section exposes an unordered-pair space.  Pivoted factor
selection gives 496 candidate columns.  To avoid the sixteen most
ill-conditioned directions, retain the 480 principal eigenmodes above
\(10^{-5}\) and scale their inverse-square-root factor by \(0.9999\).
For the nonzero/nonzero hidden-column class and nonzero translation
frequency, the checker proves

$$
\left\lVert S^TM_{J,J}S\right\rVert_{\rm op}
\le
\max_i\sum_j\left|(S^TM_{J,J}S)_{ij}\right|
\le 0.999800421220<1.
\tag{3.2}
$$

Thus \(M_{:,J}SS^TM_{J,:}\preceq M\) is an explicit PSD lower
compression.

## 4. Exact numerators and roundoff certificate

Every symmetry-block entry has common denominator

$$
Q=q^4(q-1)^4=968381956096.
\tag{4.1}
$$

The checker recovers the integer numerator matrix \(C=QM_{:,J}\); the
largest recovery residual is below \(1/64\).  It then computes
\(C^TC\) exactly with signed base-\(2^{16}\) digits.  Every short digit dot
product stays below \(2^{53}\), so those products are exact in binary64.

The remaining products use length-16 partial dots with compensated block
summation.  Entrywise input rounding and a \(32u\) multiplication allowance,
where \(u=2^{-53}\), give spectral error at most

$$
0.001400171.
\tag{4.2}
$$

After subtracting (4.2) eigenvalue by eigenvalue, the 480-dimensional factor
has trace-norm lower

$$
194.6427\ldots>194.
\tag{4.3}
$$

Only this single Fourier class is charged.  Its multiplicity is
\((q-1)^3\), so the rational lower \(194\) gives

$$
c_{\rm phys}
\ge
{N(q-1)^3\cdot194
 \over
 \sqrt{[N^2q\binom q3][N^3(q-1)^2]}}
=0.0142810242047.
\tag{4.4}
$$

All other Fourier classes are discarded.  An exploratory compression using
all eight classes gives \(0.0158103517\), but no claim relies on it.

## 5. Hybrid exact/global promise theorem

Let

$$
\psi_\beta(x)=
\log\mathbb E e^{-x(\eta-\beta)},
\qquad
L_\beta=\log{1+\beta\over1-\beta},
\qquad
\kappa_\beta={2\beta\over L_\beta}.
$$

For scalar Chernoff tilt \(s=t/\sqrt N\), martingale block \(j\) has
Euclidean coefficient norm \(\beta^j/\sqrt N\).  Use

$$
\Phi_j(s)=
\begin{cases}
\psi_\beta(s\beta^j),&s\beta^j\le L_\beta,\\
\frac12\kappa_\beta(s\beta^j)^2,&s\beta^j>L_\beta.
\end{cases}
\tag{5.1}
$$

The first branch is the proved finite-tilt Euclidean packing theorem.  The
second is the global Kearns--Saul bound.  They agree in value and derivative
at \(L_\beta\).  Iterated conditioning therefore gives, for every \(s\ge0\),

$$
\log\mathbb P(F-\beta^4\le-(\beta^4-1/4))
\le
-s\sqrt N(\beta^4-1/4)
+\sum_{j=0}^3\Phi_j(s).
\tag{5.2}
$$

Optimizing each branch interval is legitimate because every evaluated
\(s\) already supplies a valid Chernoff upper bound.  At the optimizer in
(1.4),

$$
s=2.26372145444,\qquad
2\epsilon_\beta^{\rm hybrid}=0.0204383513031,
$$

and exactly one block uses the global branch.
