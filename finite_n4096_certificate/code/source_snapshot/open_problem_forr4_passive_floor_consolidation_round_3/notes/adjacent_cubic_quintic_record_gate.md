# Adjacent cubic--quintic record gate

Date: 2026-07-15

Status: exact link formulas, proved two-split promise extension, and an exact
\(q=32\) squared-slice obstruction to the direct local contraction.  Its
compound-frame gate is now historical because a subsequent accepted-sector
repair creates a much broader passing target.  This note does not decide the
arbitrary-law coefficient, complete the one-batch ledger, or address
adaptivity.

## 1. Decision result

The newly leading split orbit is generated by

$$
(1,1,3,5):(0,1,2,2)
\quad\text{and}\quad
(1,1,3,5):(1,0,1,3),
\tag{1.1}
$$

with reversals.  The second cut is the complement of the first.  For the
first cut, rows are indexed by

$$
(b,C,D)
$$

and columns by

$$
(a,e,T),
$$

where \(a,b,e\) are single cells, \(C,D\) are pairs, and \(T\) is a triple.
The entry is

$$
M_{11}(a,b)\,
M_{13}(b,C\cup\{e\})\,
M_{35}(C\cup\{e\},D\cup T).
\tag{1.2}
$$

This reconstruction is independently checked against complete signed-
permutation enumeration at \(q=4\).

A stronger promise theorem raises the reoptimized scalar threshold from
\(0.009027152391\) to

$$
\boxed{c_*^{\rm ext}=0.011988588293.}
\tag{1.3}
$$

The exact record-one and record-three horizontal-triple slice coefficients
are

$$
c_1=0.011882208852,\qquad
c_3=0.011970102910.
\tag{1.4}
$$

Each fits (1.3) separately.  Their entrywise-disjoint squared energies add
inside the direct row-energy contraction, however, giving

$$
\boxed{c_{\rm local}=0.016866245904>c_*^{\rm ext}.}
\tag{1.5}
$$

In the full ledger, (1.5) gives \(0.333601728190\), above \(1/3\) by
\(0.000268394856\).

Decision: the separate record slices and the improved promise theorem are
accepted, but the direct local contraction is falsified.  The next bounded
Track A target is a shared physical-law/compound-frame contraction with
factor below

$$
{c_*^{\rm ext}\over c_{\rm local}}
=0.7108035992.
\tag{1.6}
$$

A physical lower witness above (1.3) would instead obstruct the scalar
completion and trigger the named joint-ledger decision.

## 2. Exact adjacent signed-permutation moments

Write a cell as \((x,c)\).  In a signed permutation link, sign parity forces
the odd column record of the left support to map to the odd row record of
the right support.

For record one, let \(c_0\) and \(r_0\) be the unique odd column and row.
After conditioning on \(\pi(c_0)=r_0\), a cubic left support has at most one
nontrivial even column character and a quintic right support has at most two
nontrivial even row characters.  Enumerating whether the even column maps
to either distinguished even row gives the closed formulas implemented by
`record_one_link_moment`.

For record three, the three odd columns must map bijectively to the three
odd rows.  Averaging the six bijections gives

$$
M_{35}(Q,R)
= {1\over 6\binom q3}
\sum_{\sigma\in S_3}
\prod_{i=1}^3
\chi_{X_i}(r_{\sigma(i)})
\chi_{c_i}(Y_{\sigma(i)}),
\tag{2.1}
$$

with an additional conditional even-row character average when the
quintic has row partition \(2+1+1+1\).

Complete \(q=4\) enumeration checks every relevant record-one and
record-three \(M_{35}\) entry exactly.

## 3. Physical fixed-orbit diagnostics

Translation orbits provide a lower-witness stress test.  Orthogonality of
\(M_{11}\) reduces the direct matrix to one block with

$$
{N^2\over4}\ \text{rows},\qquad N^2\ \text{columns}.
$$

For a representative vertical cubic pair, horizontal quintic pair, and
L-shaped triple,

$$
c(q=4)={17\over576}=0.029513888889.
$$

The largest tested symmetry-distinct \(q=8\) representative is

$$
c(q=8)={2071\over1204224}
=0.001719779709.
$$

The sharp drop rules out extrapolating the small-\(q\) obstruction.  These
are physical lower diagnostics only; they neither upper-bound mixtures nor
decide \(q=32\).

## 4. Exact horizontal record-one slice

Fix the cubic singleton cell and a triple in one row.  The cubic
record-one extensions are the \(3(q-1)^2\) L-shapes through the fixed cell.
For each fixed quintic support, summing \(M_{35}^2\) over those L-shapes
depends only on whether the quintic has zero or one nontrivial even row
group:

$$
S_0={3\over q^2},\qquad
S_1={3(q^2+1)\over q^2(q-1)^2}.
\tag{4.1}
$$

The numbers of quintic extensions of the fixed horizontal triple are

$$
\begin{aligned}
n_0&=\binom{q-3}{2}+q(q-1),\\
n_1&=(q-4)q(q-1)+(q-1)\binom q2.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.2}
$$

The first line includes the all-five-in-one-row supports and the
four-in-one-row supports whose even character has zero xor.  Therefore

$$
J_1=n_0S_0+n_1S_1.
\tag{4.3}
$$

At \(q=32\),

$$
(n_0,n_1)=(1398,43152),\qquad
J_1=138.936932963710,
\tag{4.4}
$$

and the preceding two singleton links give

$$
c_1=
\sqrt{{J_1\over q^2(q-1)^2}}
=0.0118822088518.
\tag{4.5}
$$

Direct enumeration reproduces (4.3) at \(q=4,8\).

## 5. Exact horizontal record-three slice

For the same fixed horizontal triple, a row-record-three quintic adds one
cell in each of two new rows.  Its effective odd-record cubic is an endpoint
record-three support containing one fixed cell.

The cubic supports on the other side are record-\((1,3)\) stars containing
one fixed cell.  Their incidence is

$$
D_1^{\rm star}
={(q-1)(q-2)(3q-2)\over2}.
$$

For one fixed record-three input, the exact one-cell output squared slice is

$$
C_1={3\over(q-1)(q-2)}.
$$

Consequently the complete joint Frobenius slice is

$$
J_3=D_1^{\rm star}C_1
={3(3q-2)\over2}.
\tag{5.1}
$$

The vectorized direct calculations give \(15,33,69\) at \(q=4,8,16\),
respectively, exactly matching (5.1).  At \(q=32\),

$$
J_3=141,\qquad
c_3=
\sqrt{{141\over q^2(q-1)^2}}
=0.0119701029103.
\tag{5.2}
$$

Since the two record sectors occupy disjoint entries but share partial row
and column indices, the safe local squared-slice bound is

$$
c_{\rm local}
=\sqrt{{J_1+J_3\over q^2(q-1)^2}},
\tag{5.3}
$$

which is (1.5).  Taking \(\max\{c_1,c_3\}\) would require a new compound
orthogonality theorem and is not currently justified.

## 6. Euclidean packing through the two-split gate

Let

$$
\psi_\beta(s)=
\log\mathbb E e^{-s(\eta-\beta)}
=\beta s+\log(\cosh s-\beta\sinh s).
$$

The earlier proof used exact Euclidean packing only through

$$
L_\beta=\log{1+\beta\over1-\beta}.
$$

The exact branch extends farther.  Put

$$
a_\beta(s)={\psi_\beta'(s)\over s}
={(1-\beta^2)\tanh s\over
s(1-\beta\tanh s)}.
\tag{6.1}
$$

The sign of \(a_\beta'\) is the sign of

$$
F_\beta(s)
=s\,\operatorname{sech}^2s-\tanh s+\beta\tanh^2s,
$$

and

$$
F_\beta'(s)
=2\tanh s\,\operatorname{sech}^2s\,(\beta-s).
\tag{6.2}
$$

Thus \(a_\beta\) is strictly unimodal.  A pairwise rotation at fixed
Euclidean norm then shows that an extremal coefficient vector has \(k\)
equal nonzero magnitudes.  Hence for total scalar norm \(s\),

$$
\sum_i\psi_\beta(|y_i|)
\le
\max_{k\ge1}
k\psi_\beta(s/\sqrt k).
\tag{6.3}
$$

Let \(G_\beta\) be the first root above \(L_\beta\) of

$$
\psi_\beta(G_\beta)
=2\psi_\beta(G_\beta/\sqrt2).
\tag{6.4}
$$

For \(s\le G_\beta<\sqrt2L_\beta\), monotonicity of
\(\psi_\beta(x)/x^2\) below \(L_\beta\) makes \(k=2\) the strongest
competitor to \(k=1\).  Equations (6.3)--(6.4) therefore prove

$$
\sum_i\psi_\beta(|y_i|)\le\psi_\beta(s),
\qquad 0\le s\le G_\beta.
\tag{6.5}
$$

Applying (6.5) to all four reverse-martingale blocks and optimizing
\(s\le G_\beta\) gives the new promise theorem.

With the two previous physical witness orbits fixed, reoptimization gives

$$
\beta=0.779868743331,\qquad
\text{baseline}=0.332675996818,
\qquad
\text{slack}=0.000657336515.
\tag{6.6}
$$

The common remaining-split diagnostic improves to

$$
0.00051010716305\approx {1\over1960.37}.
\tag{6.7}
$$

## 7. Scope and next gate

What is proved:

- the exact record-one and record-three \(M_{35}\) formulas;
- the direct/reduced \(q=4\) normalization and the \(q=8\) orbit diagnostic;
- the horizontal slice identities (4.3) and (5.1);
- the extended Euclidean packing theorem (6.5); and
- failure of the direct combined row-energy contraction by the amount in
  (1.5)--(1.6).

What is not proved:

- an arbitrary-law coefficient below (1.3);
- a physical \(q=32\) lower witness above (1.3);
- the remaining one-batch profiles; or
- the adaptive lift.

Historically, the next bounded calculation was to retain the two Fourier
frames under their common physical law and seek factor
\(0.7108035992\).  The exploratory mixed-law reduction and physical-family
stress test did not produce a \(q=32\) obstruction, while the candidate
type-block rank--Frobenius architecture was too loose.

More importantly, the resulting repair audit found a rigorous chained-slice
improvement in an accepted degree-eight sector.  The current live target is
to classify all unforced high-degree splits at scale \(1/q\); its coarse
ledger is \(0.322669154028\).  See
`adjacent_record_three_chained_repair.md`.  The compound factor in this note
should no longer be used as a Round 3 acceptance gate.
