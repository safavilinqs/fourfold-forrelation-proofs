## What counts as a successful round

Round 3 need not solve every track.  It should leave an integrated answer
that improves the original program in at least one major way and seriously
tests another:

- a realistic-size passive \(>6\) theorem;
- a stronger asymptotic floor or reusable posterior-stable contraction;
- a boundary-changing passive or active protocol result;
- a quantitatively better hard instance; or
- a rigorous limitation that redirects the program.

The final synthesis must state the best theorem, finite-size verdict,
active/passive boundary map, proved mechanism, and next route.
