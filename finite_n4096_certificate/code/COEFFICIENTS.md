The first family is a registry of inherited derivations, not a single new bound: follow its source imports and the recorded [source map](../dev/SOURCE_MAP.md). The 888 rows concern the balanced high sectors; lower-degree entries are added by `all_coefficient_uppers` in [the ledger assembler](source_snapshot/open_problem_forr4_passive_floor_consolidation_round_4/searches/q64_complete_outward_ledger.py). The [ledger regression](source_snapshot/open_problem_forr4_passive_floor_consolidation_round_4/tests/q64_complete_outward_ledger.py) reconstructs the frozen result, rejects unbalanced entries and verifies exact squared bounds for the residual and dual-endpoint families.

Every within-block simple-support restriction and cross-cut distinctness mask is part of the kernel. The older universal coefficient-one claims are quarantined. All files under `source_snapshot` retain historical assertions; later adaptive verdicts do not form part of this coefficient interface. See [the current audit](../../AUDIT.md).
