# Goal

Build a self-contained paper package proving the finite-size active--passive hard-dose separation for four-fold forrelation at $N=4096$ in the number-sector-incoherent fresh-batch passive class, with the exact access model, hard distribution, outward numerical certificate, adaptive lift, active protocol, physical resource statement, complete code snapshot, and reproducibility commands stated in one place.

The primary mathematical claim is

$$
D_{\mathsf P}^{\rm hard}(4096)>6,
\qquad
D_{\mathsf A}^{\rm hard}(4096)\le6,
$$

for classically adaptive passive protocols whose fresh probes commute with total signal photon number and carry no coherent inter-batch quantum memory, under branchwise hard photon-pass dose and worst-case promised error at most $1/3$.

The paper distinguishes theorem-level mathematical claims from open extensions, implementation requirements, and current-platform evidence. The finite-size theorem is certified only for number-sector-incoherent fresh probes; the broader cross-sector-coherent passive claim remains open. The $N=4096$ active implementation is specified at the resource level and is not currently certified as experimentally credible.
